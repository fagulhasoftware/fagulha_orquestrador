## Acknowledged contribution

Orquestrador Fagulha was built by three participants. The split below acknowledges each one's contribution, as
defined by the owner; it does not change ownership or the license.

| Participant | Contribution | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Fagulha Software** | **50%** | Product owner: concept, scope, security design decisions (permission levels and hybrid model), identity and brand, validation with real users, publishing and direction of every release. |
| **[Codex](https://github.com/codex)** (OpenAI) | **30%** | Extension core engineering: agent integration, MCP server, approval gate, local storage, attachments, sign-in, voice, chats and memory; automated tests, packaging and privacy check. |
| **[Claude](https://github.com/claude)** (Anthropic) | **20%** | Architecture and the UI ↔ extension contract, chat interface, documentation and translations, specifications for each release, cross-review and privacy audits. |

The AI assistants worked under the direction and approval of Fagulha Software and hold no rights to the project.
